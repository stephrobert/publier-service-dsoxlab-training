# pub-socle-service

**Audience:** the learner.

Learn to identify what actually answers behind an HTTP response, and to tell a
technical success from a business function that is down.

## What you will be able to do

- Identify the process, user and listening port that serve a response.
- Find the dependency a business function relies on.
- Observe what a restart keeps and what it loses.

Your work happens on `edge.lab`; `challenge/README.md` describes the expected
sheet. The sheet content (the acceptance criteria) is also reviewed by a human.
